# Addendum to GATE.md: the context window

Written 2026-11-10 (23:10 UTC on November 9), after the first main run was stopped and
before the rerun started. GATE.md is unchanged, and so is its rule. **Do not edit this file
after the rerun.** The rerun records its sha256 next to GATE.md's.

*Published copy: wording about the working process was removed after the runs, so this
text no longer hashes to the sha256 the campaign recorded. No prediction, bound or rule
was changed ([README](../README.md#design-and-testing)).*

## What happened

The first main run (`20261109-222742-main`) used the server flags of spec-ptc's own
campaign, which include `--max-model-len 8192`. Its first round at concurrency 8 finished
three units, then I stopped it:

| unit | tasks that failed | why |
|---|---|---|
| `nospec_c8_r1` | 6 of 8 | the root's prompt passed 8,192 tokens (HTTP 400) |
| `fanout_c8_r1` | 2 of 8 | the same |
| `stream_c8_r1` | 4 of 8 | the same three times; one task timed out |

rlms 0.1.3 tells the model that REPL output up to about 20,000 characters comes back to
it, and that a sub-call can take about 500,000 characters. OOLONG's lines run at about 2.4
characters per token, so one full REPL output is about 8,000 tokens. With an 8,192-token
window a root dies as soon as it prints much, and a sub-call can read at most about
4,000 tokens. A cell needs 12 finished tasks of 16 to count, and half of the tasks fail.

## What changes

The rerun adds one server flag: **`--max-model-len 65536`**. Everything else is as
before: the model, the other flags (`--enforce-eager` included), the tasks, the arms,
spec-ptc at 9b78b7d unmodified, the warm-up, the order (concurrency 8 first).

Why 65,536:
- rlms' own example splits a context into chunks of about 100,000 characters. For OOLONG
  text that is about 42,000 tokens, plus the 4,096 tokens a sub-call may write. 65,536 is
  the smallest power of two above that.
- The H100 has room for 154,816 tokens of KV cache with this model, so the window fits.

## What I had seen when I chose this

- The smoke run (one TREC task per arm, concurrency 1): 13 sub-calls took 1.1 s of a 118 s
  session in `nospec`.
- The three units above. In `stream_c8_r1` the four finished tasks had after-stream shares
  of 0.003, 0.081, 0.111 and 0.140. Its speculation hit 942 calls and missed 1,573.
- No result with the larger window.

So the choice of window was made knowing the 8,192-token numbers, and before any number
it produces.

## What to expect from the rerun

- Budget: about 80 minutes. That probably covers concurrency 8 only. Then "go" can be
  reached and "no-go" cannot (it needs both cells), and the verdict may stay unresolved.
- A different workload, not a repaired one. With room for long prompts the model may
  send fewer, larger sub-calls. The 8,192-token units stay in the write-up as what the
  repository's flags do.
- Known defect, left in: in streaming mode spec-ptc's shadow thread dies when it peeks
  a call whose argument is a list (`shadow.py:314`, `Counter` over an unhashable key). It
  happened twice in `stream_c8_r1`. The client is the one under test, so it stays as it is
  and the time this costs counts in the stream arm's after-stream share.
